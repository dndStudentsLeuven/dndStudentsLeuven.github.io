Deep in the forests of Arenberg Park lies a mysterious tavern where wizards and ingenious artificers, students of the laws of nature gather in the evening. Over foaming cups and amidst much laughter, they tell wild tales and roll dice! This oneshot campaign will be like no other, so grab your dice, because you all meet in the Winacafé!

📅 When: Wednesday, April 1st (Not a joke)

🕕 Time: 9 PM - 2 AM

📍 Location: Alma 3

The bar is open whether you will play or not, so if you couldn't get a spot, or just want to have a jolly good time among good company, we'd love to see you there! As usual, whether you’re a beginner taking your first steps into the world of D&D or an experienced player looking for new challenges you can register for a game during the event.

Bring your friends, meet new people, and get ready for an evening full of excitement and fun. Don’t miss out—be part of an unforgettable adventure!

What to bring? Good stories, a sense of adventure, and a set of dice (if you have one)! 🎲
